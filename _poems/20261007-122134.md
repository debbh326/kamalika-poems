poem: |-
  And Still, I Bless You
  Kamalika Bhattacharya 

  Your failure judges my character,
  Your success is their credit.

  Your pain becomes my sin,
  Your happiness, their wishes.

  A mother will take all the blame,
  Walk through the fire, endure the flame,
  And through it all,
  Always bless your name.
