---
title: Where are you
lang: en
poem: |-
  The day is going down soon,
  Where were you this afternoon?

  Through busy hours, I barely know
  How quickly the fading moments go.

  A thousand messages fill my phone,
  Yet one is all I wish to find—The one that carries you alone,

  The one my restless heart designed.
  I hide my feelings, wrapped in care,
  And call it caution, call it pride;

  Yet instinct makes my fingers dare
  To scroll and search what I conceal inside.

  The evening falls, the screen grows dim,
  But still my heart keeps asking clear:

  Among these words, among this din,
  Where are you, my dear?
