poem: |-
  Please Don't Say Bye
  Kamalika Bhattacharya 

  Don't say bye before you go,
  Its echo lingers, soft and slow.
  It sounds like a mirror when it falls,
  Its shattered echoes shake my walls.

  Please don't say bye,
  It makes me cry.
  Just leave me slowly, like a mother
  Who lays her baby down with care,
  After holding him close to her,
  With tenderness beyond compare.

  When the baby is fast asleep,
  Lost in dreams so warm and deep,
  Let me live in that belief,
  Safe from the ache of parting's grief.

  Let me not know, let me not see
  What waking from that dream could mean.
