title: A Tiny Photon

poem: |-
  A Tiny Photon
  Kamalika Bhattacharya 

  Sending the sun’s warmth to you,
  when four walls keep you glued,
  when piles of work and tension grow,
  and there’s nowhere else to go.
  When survival leaves no time
  to pause, to breathe, to feel the rhyme,
  take in one tiny photon of light—
  perhaps it will make your day bright.
  A little warmth upon your face,
  a fleeting moment, a quiet space;
  for who can say what light can do—
  perhaps it brings you back to you.
